# New Capability Requires Business Case

**[MANDATORY]**

No new business capability (e.g. a new fulfillment model, a new marketplace channel) may be architected without a documented business case including expected revenue/cost impact and the sponsoring business owner. Architecture work without a named business owner is not resourced correctly and will stall.
