# Store and Digital P&L Attribution

[RECOMMENDED]

Where a proposal affects revenue attribution between store and digital channels (e.g. BOPIS, ship-from-store), Finance must be consulted before the ADR is finalized. This is a policy, not mandatory-blocking, because Finance input can arrive after initial architecture work but must arrive before launch.
