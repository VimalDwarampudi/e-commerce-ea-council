# Capability Assessment Method

Score each capability on Business Value (H/M/L) x Technical Maturity (H/M/L) x Differentiation (Core/Enabling/Commodity). Core capabilities with low technical maturity are the highest-priority investment targets. Commodity capabilities with high maturity are decommission/buy candidates, not build targets.
