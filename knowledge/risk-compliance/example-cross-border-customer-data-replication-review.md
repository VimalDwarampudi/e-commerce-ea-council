# Cross-Border Customer Data Replication Review

Request: assess a proposal to replicate customer order data to a new regional data center for latency reasons.

Finding: proposed target region has no data processing agreement in place and the replication would include EU customer records.

Risk Rating: HIGH — cross-border transfer without adequate safeguards.

Recommendation: escalate per the Regulatory Uncertainty trigger in the escalation matrix; require Legal to confirm an adequate transfer mechanism (SCCs or equivalent) before this proceeds, or scope the replication to exclude EU customer records.
