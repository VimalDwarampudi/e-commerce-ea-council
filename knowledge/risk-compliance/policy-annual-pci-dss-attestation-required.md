# Annual PCI-DSS Attestation Required

**[MANDATORY]**

The organization must maintain current PCI-DSS attestation of compliance (SAQ or full assessment, depending on transaction volume) at all times; any architecture change expanding cardholder data scope resets the assessment clock and must be flagged before implementation, not discovered at annual audit.
