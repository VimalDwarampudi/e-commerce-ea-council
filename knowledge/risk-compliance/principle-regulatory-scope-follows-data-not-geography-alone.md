# Regulatory Scope Follows Data, Not Geography Alone

A retailer serving customers across jurisdictions must apply the most stringent applicable regulation to a given data flow by default (e.g. GDPR-equivalent handling for any EU customer data regardless of where systems are hosted), rather than trying to maintain separate compliance postures per region for shared systems.
