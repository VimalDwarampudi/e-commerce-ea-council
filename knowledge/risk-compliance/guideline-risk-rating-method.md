# Risk Rating Method

Rate identified risks as LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/CRITICAL based on Likelihood x Impact, where Impact considers both direct financial exposure and regulatory/reputational exposure. CRITICAL ratings trigger mandatory escalation per the escalation matrix regardless of likelihood.
