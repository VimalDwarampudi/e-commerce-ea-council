# OMS Is the System of Record for Order Status

**[MANDATORY]**

Store POS, the digital storefront, customer service tools, and shipping carriers all read order status from the OMS; none may maintain an independent order status of record. This directly parallels the Catalog domain's PIM policy and exists for the same reason — status divergence is a top driver of customer complaints.
