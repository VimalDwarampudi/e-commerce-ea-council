# Order State Transitions Are Auditable

Every order status transition must be logged with timestamp, actor (system or human), and reason. Order status is a common source of customer service escalations and regulatory inquiry (e.g. delivery guarantee disputes) — the audit trail is not optional instrumentation, it's a requirement.
