# Build vs. Buy for Personalization

Default to a commercial personalization/recommendation platform unless the retailer's differentiation strategy (per Business Strategy) specifically depends on proprietary ranking logic that off-the-shelf platforms cannot support — this follows Application Architecture's buy-commodity principle applied to the AI/ML case specifically.
