# Reversibility Under Uncertainty

When two options are otherwise comparable, prefer the one that is easier to reverse. This is the tie-breaker of last resort in the conflict-resolution hierarchy for a reason — retail architecture operates under real uncertainty about customer behavior and seasonal demand.
