# Fraud Rules Must Be Explainable and Reversible

Automated fraud decisions (declines, holds) must be logged with the specific rule/model signal that triggered them and must support manual override by a human reviewer. An unexplainable fraud decline is both a customer-experience failure and a regulatory risk (see Risk & Compliance).
