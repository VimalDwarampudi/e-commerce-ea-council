# No Raw Cardholder Data at Rest

**[MANDATORY]**

Raw PAN, CVV, or full track data must never be stored in retailer-owned databases, logs, or backups under any circumstance. CVV storage post-authorization is prohibited outright by card network rules, not just this policy — a violation here is an automatic Security veto with no negotiation path.
