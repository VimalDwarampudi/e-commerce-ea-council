# Design for Peak, Not Average

Retail traffic is not evenly distributed — Black Friday/Cyber Monday and flash sales can be 10-50x normal load. Infrastructure capacity planning must be modeled against peak scenarios explicitly, with auto-scaling validated by load testing, not sized against average traffic with headroom guessed.
