# Cloud Region Strategy

Primary customer-facing systems should run multi-AZ at minimum; multi-region is warranted for systems where an outage directly halts revenue (checkout, payments, POS backend) but may be excessive for internal/back-office systems — apply cost proportional to revenue impact, not uniformly.
