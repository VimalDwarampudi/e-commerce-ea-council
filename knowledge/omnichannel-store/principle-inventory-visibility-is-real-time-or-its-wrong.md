# Inventory Visibility Is Real-Time or It's Wrong

BOPIS and endless-aisle features are only as trustworthy as inventory freshness. A promise of in-store availability based on stale (e.g. nightly-batch) inventory data will generate broken promises to customers at the point of pickup — this is treated as a functional defect, not a performance nice-to-have.
