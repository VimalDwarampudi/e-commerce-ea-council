# Search Platform Evaluation Criteria

Evaluate candidate search platforms against: relevance tuning flexibility, faceted navigation performance at catalog scale, personalization hook availability (for Data & AI integration), peak-load query throughput (Black Friday scale), and total cost of ownership including relevance-tuning labor, not just licensing.
