# Zero-Result Query Monitoring

[RECOMMENDED]

Any search platform must expose zero-result-rate as a monitored metric with alerting. A rising zero-result rate is a leading indicator of catalog or taxonomy problems and must be visible to Catalog & Product Information, not just Search & Discovery.
