# No PII in Search Query Logs Without Redaction

**[MANDATORY]**

Search query logs must not retain personally identifiable information (e.g. a customer typing their own email or address into search) beyond what is required for abuse detection, and must be redacted or purged per the retention policy in Risk & Compliance's data policies.
