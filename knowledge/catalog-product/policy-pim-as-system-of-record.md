# PIM as System of Record

**[MANDATORY]**

No system other than the designated PIM may be treated as authoritative for product title, description, attributes, or category assignment. Point-to-point product data sync between two non-PIM systems is prohibited — all product data flows through the PIM and its published integration contract.
