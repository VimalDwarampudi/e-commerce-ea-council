# PIM vs. DAM Boundary

Structured product attributes (price, size, material, compliance data) live in the PIM. Unstructured assets (images, video, 360-degree spins) live in a Digital Asset Management system and are referenced, not embedded, from PIM records. Do not let either system grow to duplicate the other's responsibility.
